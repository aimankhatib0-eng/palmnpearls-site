The house

Fine cars, chosen with care.

PNP Motors, short for Palm and Pearls Motors, offers a small, hand-picked collection of very premium pre-owned cars to buyers between Dubai and India. We say no to most cars.

What we do

We select premium cars from verified partner dealers, check them, document them and present them in private. Each car is shown only once it meets our standard.

For whom

Buyers who value certainty over volume: business owners, collectors, executives and families who want a fine car without the guesswork of the used-car market.

Where

Dubai and the GCC, and India, with Mumbai and Navi Mumbai as our home market. We understand both sides of the journey.

Our position

For buyers who want a fine car without the uncertainty, PNP Motors is the private house that hand-picks and verifies every car before it is shown, so what you see is what you drive.

The PNP standard

Verified before it is shown.

Five things a car must earn before we show it to you. If a car falls short on any of them, it does not reach the collection.

  1. Provenance

    Known owners, documented history and verifiable mileage.

    Owners · history · odometer

  2. Condition

    Inspected, with every finding recorded in a written condition report.

    Exterior · interior · mechanical

  3. Paperwork

    Clear title, with the papers explained to you before any commitment.

    Title · registration · service book

  4. Presentation

    Shown clean, calm and properly photographed. The real car, in honest light.

    Six views of every car

  5. Transparency

    One price, with no surprises behind it. Ask us anything.

    One price · one conversation

How buying works

A clean, explained process.

Four steps, at your pace. You deal with one advisor from the first message to the day you collect your keys.

  1. 01

    Tell us the car

    Share what you are looking for, by message, call or in person. Or choose one from the collection.

  2. 02

    We hand-pick and verify

    We source from verified partner dealers, inspect the car and record the findings in a condition report.

  3. 03

    Private viewing

    See the car by appointment in Dubai or Navi Mumbai, or on a live video walk-around from wherever you are.

  4. 04

    Papers and handover

    Clear title, explained before you commit. You receive an olive wallet with the papers, the printed condition report and a handwritten note.

Buying from afar

For buyers overseas and between Dubai and India: clear photographs of the real car, the full document set and honest answers, with a video walk-around whenever you want one. You can decide with confidence without making the trip.
